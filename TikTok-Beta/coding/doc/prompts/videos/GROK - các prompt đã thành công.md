## 4. Camera di chuyển vòng quanh người mẫu

### Video 10s - **Checked**

> Nhớ kiểm tra bối cảnh xem có cần bổ sung gì vào prompt hay ko, ví dụ như người mẫu cần nhìn vào điện thoại xuyên suốt video, hoặc ko để bàn chân của người mẫu lọt vào khung hình video, hoặc là cần gắn thêm ảnh tham chiếu trang phục vào ô nhập prompt để giữ nguyên trang phục xuyên suốt video, v.v.

Use the person from the reference image as the main subject. Generate a realistic video with a fast, smooth orbital camera movement around the model. The camera must keep a constant distance from the model during the entire shot. Do not zoom in, do not zoom out, do not move closer, and do not move farther away. Keep the same lens, same camera height, and stable full-body framing throughout the video.

Camera path:

- Start with the camera facing the model directly from the front.
- Then move quickly in an orbit to the model’s right side.
- Then continue orbiting to the back of the model.
- Then continue moving around to the model’s right side again.
- Then move around to the model’s left side.
- Finally return to the front, ending with the camera directly facing the model.

Subject behavior:

- The model remains completely still for the entire video.
- Keep the exact same pose, body position, and facial expression throughout.
- No walking, no turning, no arm movement, no hand movement, no body sway.
- Only very subtle, natural eye movement is allowed.

Visual requirements:

- Fast camera orbit, smooth, stable, continuous motion.
- No scene cuts, no scene transitions.
- No camera shake.
- No face distortion.
- Preserve the original appearance, outfit, proportions, lighting, and background.
- Clean, minimal background.
- Realistic motion and natural rendering.
